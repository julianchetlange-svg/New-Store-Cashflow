Model Controls
Advanced — duration & sequencing assumptions (currently: compressed target schedule, not historical average)

Historical average across the four benchmark stores ran ~26 months design-side and ~21 months construction-side (see Section 03). The defaults below compress that to a 6-month design/permitting window followed sequentially by 10 months of construction — a 16-month total build, roughly 40% shorter than what these four stores actually took. Applying the same historical spend curve to a shorter timeline concentrates the same dollars into fewer months, so expect a materially higher peak monthly burn than the benchmark projects saw.
